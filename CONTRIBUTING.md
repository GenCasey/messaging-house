# Contributing

Thanks for looking at this. Two kinds of contribution are most useful.

**Briefs where it went wrong.** If you ran the skill on a real product and the output invented a feature, picked a why-buy your customer would not recognise, or dropped a fact, open an issue with the brief you gave it (anonymised if you need to) and what came out. Failures on real products are how the method improves.

**Method improvements.** The skill lives in `skills/messaging-house/`. `SKILL.md` is the workflow; `references/` holds the full method, the interview questions and the worked example. Keep `SKILL.md` under about 2,500 words and push detail into the references. Examples should be fictional and, by default, software or service businesses.

Before opening a pull request, run the eval briefs in `skills/messaging-house/evals/evals.json` with and without your change and include what moved. The checklist for each brief is in the same file.

Plain language throughout. Short sentences. No em dashes.
